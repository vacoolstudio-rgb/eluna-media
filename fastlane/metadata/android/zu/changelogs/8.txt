Inguqulo 1.0 — ukukhishwa kokuqala.

• Guqula ucindezele amavidiyo, izithombe nomsindo kule divayisi kuphela.
• Linganisa ngobukhulu: khetha u-10, 16, 25, 50 noma 100 MB, umphumela uwele ngaphansi.
• Ulayini wamafayela amaningi oqhubekayo ngemuva futhi osinda ekuqaleni kabusha.
• Nquma, phendula, hlanganisa, shintsha ijubane, khipha i-MP3, yenza i-GIF.
• I-EXIF ne-GPS kususwa ngokuzenzakalelayo. Ayikho imvume ye-inthanethi.
• Izilimi ezingu-61, ukuma kwethebulethi, ukubukeka kwe-OLED.
