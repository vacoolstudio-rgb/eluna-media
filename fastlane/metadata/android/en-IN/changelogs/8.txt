Version 1.0 — the first public release.

• Compress and convert video, photos and audio fully offline.
• Reduce a video to an exact 10, 16, 25, 50 or 100 MB — the result lands under it.
• Resize a photo to a size in KB for online form uploads.
• Video to MP3, and free up storage by deleting the originals.
• Batch queue that runs in the background and survives a restart.
• Trim, rotate, crop, merge, change speed, make GIFs.
• EXIF and GPS removed by default. No internet permission in this build.
