Version 1.0 — the first public release.

• Convert and compress video, photos and audio entirely on the device.
• Fit to size: pick 10, 16, 25, 50 or 100 MB and the result lands under it.
• Batch queue that keeps running in the background and survives a restart.
• Trim, rotate, crop, merge, change speed, extract audio, build GIFs.
• EXIF and GPS stripped by default. No network permission in this build.
• 61 languages, tablet layouts, light, dark and OLED themes.
